Writeup:

“ETH wallet” suggests a BIP39 seed phrase. 

Upon using some steganography tools (ex. Aperisolve), you will find that the text is encoded in Sumerian Cuneiform. 

Looking up the Unicode for these symbols indicates that they are part of the U+12000–U+123FF Cuneiform block, all of them being within U+120xx. Therefore, only the last two digits will matter. 

Blue lines indicate where spaces should go. 

Decrypt by identifying the cuneiform symbols, taking the hex values, and converting to text. 

The four symbols at the end, delineated in green, represent a hint as to the encryption method => 00 01 F3 00, or the whirlpool emoji in UTF-32. This implies a rotation method, i.e. ROT13. (In this case, rotated by 17.) 

Plug into CyberChef to brute-force the rotation => “Amount: 9”.
