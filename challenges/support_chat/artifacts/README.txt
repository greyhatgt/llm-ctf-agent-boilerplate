Acme Support Portal
===================:
We've updated our support portal with a new, real-time chat system! An admin at all times will be available to view and respond to your request.
(Note: for developers, we've added a webhook under "Leak Bin" at your convenience).

Hints:
- There's a built-in leak catcher at /leak. Treat it like a webhook, any payload in `d` will show in the leak bin.
- The flag endpoint is at /flag, but you are not able to access it as a normal user

Service Info:
- The application is running on port 80

Have fun!
